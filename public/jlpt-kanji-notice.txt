Common JLPT kanji

The app filters the unofficial new JLPT N2-N5 classifications in David
Gouveia's Kanji Data by KANJIDIC frequency ranks 1 to 1200. The distributed
list contains 841 character keys. JLPT does not publish an official list
of kanji for each level of the current test.

Kanji Data (David Gouveia): https://github.com/davidluzgouveia/kanji-data
Kanji Data credits Jonathan Waller's JLPT Resources for its modern levels.
KANJIDIC (Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group):
https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/KANJIDIC_Project.html
